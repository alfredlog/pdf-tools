    How it works: PDF to Word

    1. Select a PDF

      Choose the PDF you want to edit.

    2. Convert

      Click “Convert to Word”. Depending on the number of pages, this takes a few seconds.

    3. Download

      Open the DOCX file in Word, LibreOffice or Google Docs and edit it.

    What is carried over?

    The conversion detects paragraphs, headings, tables and images and builds a Word document with real flowing text. Font sizes, bold text and colors are kept. Elaborate layouts with many columns or text boxes may need some touching up.

    Important for scanned PDFs

    To a computer, a scanned page is just a photo. Without text recognition (OCR) it ends up as an image in the Word document and the text can’t be edited. You can tell whether your PDF contains real text by trying to select and copy text in it.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I convert scanned PDFs?

    The conversion works, but scanned pages appear as images. For editable text, run text recognition first with “Make PDF searchable (OCR)”.

    Does it work with Google Docs?

    Yes. Upload the DOCX file to Google Drive and open it with Google Docs.

    Why isn’t the layout 100 % identical?

    PDFs store the exact position of every character, Word works with flowing text. With complex layouts the conversion has to make compromises.

    Is my PDF stored?

    No, it is deleted right after the conversion.

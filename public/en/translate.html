    How it works: Translate PDF

    1. Select a PDF

      Choose the document you want to translate – up to 30 pages.

    2. Choose the language

      Choose the target language. The source language is detected automatically.

    3. Translate

      The AI translates each text block and puts it in the same place. Longer translations are set slightly smaller automatically.

    4. Download

      Download the translated PDF. With Pro: unlimited.

    Translate without destroying the layout

    If you copy a PDF into a translation program, you lose tables, images and formatting. Here the page stays as it is: only the text is replaced. That works well for letters, forms, manuals, certificates you want to understand and documents for family or colleagues.

    Note: for official documents, authorities usually require a certified translation. The AI translation helps you understand a document but doesn’t replace one.

    Frequently asked questions

    How much does the translation cost?

    Translate PDF is part of PDF Libre Pro: try it free for 14 days, then by subscription or day pass.

    Which languages are available?

    German, English, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Romanian, Russian and Ukrainian.

    How many pages can I translate?

    Up to 30 pages at once. Split larger documents first with “Split PDF”.

    What happens to my document?

    The text is sent encrypted to the AI service, used only for the translation and not stored. The file is deleted right afterwards.

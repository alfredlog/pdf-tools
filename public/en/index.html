    Frequently asked questions

    Is PDF Libre free?

    Almost everything: 34 tools such as merge, compress, sign and convert are free and work without sign-up. Only “PDF bearbeiten”, “PDF erstellen”, “Summarize PDF” and “Translate PDF” are part of PDF Libre Pro: try it free for 14 days, then 4,99 € per month or 2,99 € for a day pass. No ads or watermarks are ever added to your files.

    What happens to my files?

    Tools marked “In your browser” process your file directly on your device – it is never uploaded. With all other tools the file is transferred encrypted and deleted automatically right after processing.

    Do I need to install a program or an app?

    No. PDF Libre runs in every modern browser – on Windows, Mac, Linux, Android and iPhone.

    How large may my files be?

    Up to 50 MB per file for tools that run on the server. Browser tools are only limited by your device’s memory.

    Install PDF Libre as an app

    Open PDF Libre like an app from your home screen – and on your phone, send files straight to PDF Libre via “Share”.

    1. Android (Chrome)Menu ⋮ top right → “Install app” or “Add to home screen”.
    2. iPhone and iPad (Safari)Share icon (square with arrow) → “Add to Home Screen”.
    3. Computer (Chrome or Edge)Click the “Install” icon (screen with arrow) in the address bar – or menu ⋮ → “Cast, save and share” → “Install page as app”.
    4. FirefoxFirefox can’t install web apps on a computer. Use Chrome or Edge – on Android, Firefox can do it via the menu.

    May PDF Libre analyse how the site is used?

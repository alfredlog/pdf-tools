        How it works: Create a CV

        1. Enter or import your details

          Fill in the form, load an example – or import your current CV as PDF or Word file. The AI transfers everything into the form automatically.

        2. Choose a design

          In the “Design” step you see your CV in all four templates – Modern, Classic, Academic in LaTeX style and Compact – and pick a color and language.

        3. Check and improve

          The ATS check shows what’s missing – one click jumps to the right field. The AI assistant checks spelling, improves wording and tailors your CV to a job ad.

        4. Download the PDF

          Download your CV. With “Save draft” you also get a small file to keep editing later.

        What does ATS-friendly mean?

        Many companies – from large corporations to mid-sized firms – first read applications with an applicant tracking system (ATS). The software extracts name, contact details, experience and skills from the PDF and ranks by keywords. CVs with several columns, text in graphics, icons instead of words or exotic fonts are often read incorrectly or not at all.

        That’s why PDF Libre templates use a single column, real selectable text with properly embedded fonts, standard headings such as “Experience”, “Education” and “Skills”, and a consistent date format. Your CV looks good to people and is unambiguous for machines.

        CV templates: Modern, Classic, LaTeX style and Compact

        “Modern” looks fresh with an accent color of your choice – great for IT, marketing, start-ups and student jobs. “Classic” with a serif font suits banking, administration, law and industry. “Academic” sets your CV in Latin Modern, the font known from LaTeX – ideal for research, PhD positions, scholarships and technical degrees, without any LaTeX knowledge. “Compact” fits a lot of content on one page.

        CV structure and order

        The most common format is reverse chronological: the most recent position first. A good structure: contact details, short profile, experience (including internships and student jobs), education, projects, skills, languages, volunteering and interests. For graduates, pupils and students, education may come before experience – you can change the order of sections with the arrows.

        For each position, briefly describe what you did and achieved – ideally with numbers (“Automated reports, saving 6 hours a week”). One to two pages are almost always enough.

        Import your old CV and fix it with AI

        Already have a CV in Word, as PDF or from another builder? Click “Import CV”: the text is extracted – even from scans via text recognition – and the AI moves it into the right fields. It only takes over what is in your CV and invents nothing. In a few minutes your old CV gets a modern, ATS-friendly design.

        With “Check spelling” the AI finds typos, spelling and grammar mistakes. Every correction is shown first – red for the original, green for the suggestion – and you decide one by one what to accept.

        Tailor your CV to the job ad – with a match score

        Paste a job ad under “Tailor to a job ad”. The AI extracts the requirements – programming languages, tools, degrees, languages – and shows which ones your CV already covers. This gives a match score in percent, similar to how an ATS calculates it. You can add missing terms to your skills with one click – but only if you really have them.

        The AI also suggests how to phrase your headline, profile and positions so that the relevant experience stands out immediately. Invented details are excluded: suggestions with numbers that aren’t in your CV are discarded automatically.

        Cover letter in the same design – write it yourself or with AI

        A CV usually comes with a cover letter. Under “Create cover letter” PDF Libre sets your letter in the same font and color as your CV – with recipient address, place and date, subject and closing. Write it yourself or let the AI draft it from your CV and the job ad, then make it personal.

        Applying internationally? The AI translates your whole CV into English, French or German with one click. Headings exist in all three languages: “Experience”, “Expérience professionnelle”, “Berufserfahrung”.

        CV with or without a photo?

        It depends on the country: in the UK, the US or Canada a photo is usually left out, in France, Germany or Belgium it is optional and still common in some industries. With PDF Libre you decide: add a photo or simply leave it out.

        Edit your CV later – without an account

        Your draft is saved automatically in your browser. With “Save draft” you also download a small draft file. Open it later via “Import CV” – all fields are filled in immediately, even on another device. That way you quickly adapt your CV to every new application.

        The PDF you send to companies, on the other hand, only contains what you see: no hidden data, no attachments, no hidden sections.

        Frequently asked questions

        Is the CV maker free?

        Yes. All templates, the live preview, the ATS check and the PDF download are free, without sign-up and without watermark.

        Is the CV ATS-friendly?

        Yes. It uses a single column, real text with embedded fonts and standard headings. Applicant tracking systems reliably read name, contact details, experience and skills.

        Can I create my CV in English, French or German?

        Yes. In the “Design” step choose the language – headings such as “Experience”, “Expérience professionnelle” or “Berufserfahrung” are adapted automatically. You write your texts yourself or let the AI translate them.

        How do I edit my CV later?

        Use “Save draft” to download the draft file and open it later via “Import CV”. All details go back into the form. In the same browser your draft is kept anyway.

        Does the PDF contain hidden data?

        No. The PDF for your application only contains the visible CV – no attachments and no hidden sections.

        Is my data stored?

        No. Your details are only sent to the server to create the PDF and deleted immediately. The draft is stored only in your browser.

        Can I reuse my old CV?

        Yes. With “Import CV” you upload a PDF or Word file; the AI transfers all details into the form. Then quickly check that everything is in the right place.

        Does the AI invent experience or skills?

        No. It only rewords what is in your CV. Suggestions with new numbers are discarded automatically, and you confirm every change.

        How does the match score work?

        The AI reads the requirements from the job ad. Then it checks which of them appear in your CV; required items count double. The score updates immediately when you add something.

        Does the spell check change my content?

        No. It only corrects spelling, grammar and punctuation. You see every change first and decide yourself.

        How do I add a cover letter and certificates?

        Upload your finished CV together with your cover letter and certificates in “Job application PDF” – everything is merged into one PDF there.

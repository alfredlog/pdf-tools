    How it works: Reduce application PDF size

    1. Upload your application

      Upload your finished application PDF – or all the individual documents at once.

    2. Choose the target size

      Choose “Under 2 MB” or “Under 5 MB”, depending on what the portal or job ad requires.

    3. Download the small PDF

      The file is reduced step by step until it fits under the limit and is ready to upload.

    How large may an application PDF be?

    Most application portals allow 2 to 5 MB per file, some only 1 MB. For an application by email, about 3 to 5 MB is considered polite – larger attachments end up in spam more often or are rejected by the mail server. A complete application with cover letter, CV and certificates normally fits easily into 1 to 3 MB.

    Why is my application so large?

    Almost always because of scanned certificates or very high-resolution phone photos, and large application photos. A single smartphone photo can be 5 MB. PDF Libre reduces exactly these images until the file fits your limit – fonts and lines stay sharp and searchable.

    Reduce the size without it looking bad

    Images are only reduced as much as necessary. For certificates, 150 dpi is completely sufficient – you can’t see a difference on screen or in print. Still, take a quick look at the end to check that stamps and signatures are easy to read.

    Frequently asked questions

    How do I get my application under 2 MB?

    Upload the application, choose “Under 2 MB” and click “Create application PDF”. The images in the PDF are reduced automatically until the file is smaller than 2 MB.

    Does quality suffer?

    Text, lines and fonts stay perfectly sharp. Only photos and scans are reduced in resolution – just enough that you won’t notice it when reading.

    Can I also reduce a normal PDF?

    Yes. For any other PDF, “Compress PDF” is the best choice.

    What if the portal only allows 1 MB?

    Choose “Under 2 MB” and check the size. If that’s not enough, upload certificates as black-and-white scans instead of color photos – that saves the most space.

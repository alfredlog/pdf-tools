Your complete application in four steps

A convincing application in Germany almost always consists of one single PDF file: cover letter, CV and certificates – neatly sorted, easy to read and small enough for any application portal. With PDF Libre you first create your CV (or import your old one), tailor it to the job ad, write the cover letter in the same design and finally combine everything with the application PDF tool – automatically under 5 MB.

Whether apprenticeship (Ausbildung), internship, working student job (Werkstudent), mini-job, dual study programme, first job or job change: the tools work on PC, Mac and phone and produce documents that both recruiters and applicant tracking systems (ATS) read easily. Ideal for international students and professionals applying in Germany.

Checklist before sending

  • CV tailored to the position, most important experience at the top, consistent dates (MM/YYYY).
  • Cover letter with the contact person’s name, the correct position and the reference number.
  • All documents in one PDF, in the right order, easy to read and under the size limit.
  • A meaningful file name, e.g. “Application_Jane_Doe.pdf”.
  • Spelling checked – ideally by a second person as well.

Frequently asked questions

What belongs in a complete job application in Germany?

A cover letter (Anschreiben), a CV (Lebenslauf) and the matching proof: job references, degree certificates, other certificates. A cover page is optional. Online, everything is usually sent as one single PDF.

Is a CV made with PDF Libre ATS-friendly?

Yes. The templates use a single column, real text with embedded fonts and standard headings, so applicant tracking systems read every detail correctly.

How much does it cost?

CV, cover letter, application PDF and all PDF tools are free. Only some AI features (improving wording, matching with the job ad, AI cover letter, translation) are part of PDF Libre Pro.

Are my application documents stored?

No. Files and details are only processed and deleted right afterwards. Your CV draft stays in your browser only.

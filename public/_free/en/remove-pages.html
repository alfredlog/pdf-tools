How it works: Remove pages

  1. Select a PDF

    Load the PDF into the box. All pages appear as thumbnails.

  2. Mark pages

    Click the pages you want to delete, or type in the page numbers.

  3. Remove

    Click “Remove pages”. The new PDF is created in your browser.

  4. Download

    Save the cleaned-up PDF. The original on your device stays unchanged.

Typical use cases

Scanned documents often contain blank back pages, presentations have slides that aren’t relevant for a particular recipient, and bank statements include pages with ads. Instead of recreating the file, you remove exactly the pages you don’t need.

Select pages by click or by number

Marked pages get a red frame. For long documents typing is faster: “2, 5-7” removes pages 2, 5, 6 and 7. You can combine both ways.

Frequently asked questions

Can I undo the removal?

Your original file is never changed. If you clicked the wrong page, simply load the original again.

Are bookmarks and links kept?

Page contents are kept completely. Bookmarks that point to deleted pages naturally no longer work.

Is my PDF uploaded?

No. Removing pages happens completely in your browser.

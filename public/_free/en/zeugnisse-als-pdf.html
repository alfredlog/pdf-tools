    How it works: Certificates as PDF

    1. Select photos

      Choose the photos or scans of your certificates and diplomas (JPG or PNG).

    2. Set the order

      Sort the pages – for example, the most recent certificate first.

    3. Download the PDF

      All images go into one PDF that you can upload, send by email or add to your application PDF.

    How to photograph certificates properly

    Place the certificate on a dark, plain surface and photograph it straight from above in daylight, without flash. Make sure all four corners are visible and no shadows fall across the text. That way grades, stamps and signatures stay easy to read.

    Certificates for job, university applications and recognition

    Whether job reference, school leaving certificate, bachelor’s degree, language certificate (e.g. Goethe, TestDaF, DSH, IELTS) or internship confirmation: many portals – from companies to uni-assist and German authorities – require proof as PDF. You can bundle several certificates in one file or create a separate PDF for each document.

    Add certificates to your application PDF

    You can combine the finished certificate PDF directly with your cover letter and CV into one application PDF. There the file is also reduced automatically to under 5 MB if the photos are very large.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is a phone photo enough instead of a scan?

    For most online applications, yes, if the photo is straight, sharp and well lit. For official purposes a certified copy may be required – that depends on the institution.

    How many certificates can I put into one PDF?

    As many as you like. Each image becomes its own page.

    The PDF is too large – what now?

    Add it to the application PDF tool and choose a target size, or use “Compress PDF”.

    Are my certificates stored?

    No. The conversion runs directly in your browser, your photos never leave your device.

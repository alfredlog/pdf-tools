    Do you receive e-invoices regularly?

    E-Rechnung Agent receives them automatically, checks them and prepares them for your tax advisor (DATEV) – with its own email address for invoices. Try it free for 30 days.

    See E-Rechnung Agent

    How it works: Validate e-invoice

    1. Select a file

      Choose the e-invoice – whether received or created yourself.

    2. Check

      Click “Check invoice”. Amounts and mandatory fields are verified.

    3. Read the report

      You see the result immediately, the details are in the downloaded PDF.

    What is checked?

    The tool recalculates the invoice: do the line items add up to the total, does the VAT match the tax rate, does net plus tax give the gross amount, and is the amount due correct? It also checks mandatory details such as invoice number, date and the issuer’s tax number, and verifies the IBAN for typos.

    The full check against the official XRechnung rules is done with the KoSIT validator when it is available on the server. The report shows whether this check was carried out.

    When do I have to send e-invoices in Germany?

    All companies in Germany have had to be able to receive e-invoices since 1 January 2025. For sending: companies with more than €800,000 turnover in the previous year from 1 January 2027, all others from 1 January 2028. Exceptions include small invoices up to €250 and invoices to private individuals. If in doubt, ask your tax advisor.

    Frequently asked questions

    Does this replace the check by my tax advisor?

    No. The tool finds formal and arithmetic errors. Whether an invoice is correct for tax purposes is for your tax advisor to judge.

    Can I also check a normal PDF invoice?

    No. A normal PDF contains no structured data. Supported are XRechnung (XML) and ZUGFeRD/Factur-X PDFs.

    What does “note” mean in the report?

    A note is not an error but should be looked at – for example a missing tax number or a rounding difference.

    Are my invoices stored?

    No. The file is only used for the check on our server in Germany and deleted immediately afterwards.

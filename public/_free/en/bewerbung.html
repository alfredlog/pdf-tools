Your complete application in four steps

A convincing application today almost always consists of one single PDF file: cover letter, CV and certificates – neatly sorted, easy to read and small enough for any application portal. With PDF Libre you first create your CV (or import your old one), tailor it to the job ad, write the cover letter in the same design and finally combine everything with the application PDF tool – automatically under 5 MB.

Whether apprenticeship, internship, student job, part-time job, graduate programme, first job or career change: the tools work on PC, Mac and phone and produce documents that both recruiters and applicant tracking systems (ATS) read easily.

Checklist before sending

  • CV tailored to the position, most important experience at the top, consistent dates (MM/YYYY).
  • Cover letter with the contact person’s name, the correct position and the reference number.
  • All documents in one PDF, in the right order, easy to read and under the size limit.
  • A meaningful file name, e.g. “Application_Jane_Doe.pdf”.
  • Spelling checked – ideally by a second person as well.

Frequently asked questions

What belongs in a complete job application?

A cover letter, a CV and the matching proof: references, degree certificates, other certificates. A cover page is optional. Online, everything is usually sent as one single PDF.

Is a CV made with PDF Libre ATS-friendly?

Yes. The templates use a single column, real text with embedded fonts and standard headings, so applicant tracking systems read every detail correctly.

How much does it cost?

All job application tools are free and work without sign-up.

Are my application documents stored?

No. Files and details are only processed and deleted right afterwards. Your CV draft stays in your browser only.

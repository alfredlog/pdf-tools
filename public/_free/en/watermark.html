    How it works: Watermark

    1. Select a PDF

      Choose the PDF that should get a watermark.

    2. Enter the text

      Type the text, e.g. “DRAFT” or “Only for my application at Company X”.

    3. Adjust the look

      Choose layout, opacity, size and color.

    4. Save

      Click “Add watermark” and download the PDF.

    Protect ID copies and documents

    If you have to send copies of your ID, certificates or contracts, a watermark stating the purpose and recipient helps – for example “Copy for flat application at Example Ltd.”. If the copy ends up in the wrong hands, it is much harder to misuse for other purposes.

    The option “Across the whole page” repeats the text several times so it can’t simply be cropped away.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can the watermark be removed again?

    The watermark is written into the page as text. Special software could remove it – for real protection, combine it with “Protect PDF”.

    Are special characters supported?

    Yes, Latin letters including accents and umlauts such as é, ü or ß are displayed correctly.

    Is my PDF uploaded?

    No, the watermark is added in your browser.

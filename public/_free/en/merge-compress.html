    How it works: Merge & compress

    1. Select PDFs

      Choose all files that should go into the document.

    2. Sort

      Bring the files into the right order by drag & drop.

    3. Choose a level

      Choose how strongly the finished PDF should be reduced.

    4. Download

      Click “Merge & compress” and save the result.

    Frequently asked questions

    Can I upload just one file?

    Yes, then it is only compressed – just like with “Compress PDF”.

    How many files are possible?

    Up to 20 PDFs of up to 50 MB each.

    Are the files stored?

    No, they are deleted right after processing.
